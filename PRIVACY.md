# Privacy Policy — CitizenPass

_Last updated: 2026-08-17_

CitizenPass does not collect, transmit, sell, or share any personal information.

## What CitizenPass stores

- Language preference (English/French)
- Theme preference (light/dark/system)
- Study progress and chapter completion
- Practice and exam history
- Bookmarked questions

All of the above is stored **locally on your device** using on-device storage (AsyncStorage and/or a local SQLite database). None of this data leaves your device. CitizenPass has no backend server and makes no network requests for its core functionality.

## No accounts

CitizenPass does not require sign-in, registration, or any account.

## No tracking

CitizenPass does not include analytics SDKs, advertising SDKs, or any third-party tracking.

## Data removal

You can erase all locally stored data at any time from **Settings → Reset local data**, or by uninstalling the app.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be raised via the project repository: https://github.com/tnhmar/CitizenPass
